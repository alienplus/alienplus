## 👋 About

I'm a **Forward Deployed AI Engineer** who embeds with clients to turn ambiguous business problems into production **LLM, RAG, and agentic systems**. I own delivery end to end: stakeholder discovery, technical proposals, architecture, implementation, deployment (Docker, CI/CD), and evaluation.

I've shipped AI products and internal platforms for international clients, from real-time computer vision for construction-tech to an AI CV chatbot for a startup. I work with a strong Python foundation, an AI-assisted engineering workflow, and a habit of mentoring engineers and teaching.
